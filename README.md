# Karoo Health Connect 🚴‍♂️📲

A lightweight Android companion app that seamlessly syncs rides from your Hammerhead / SRAM Karoo to Google Health Connect.

### Key Highlights
- **Direct Cloud Sync:** Pulls completed rides directly from your Hammerhead/SRAM Dashboard via OAuth.
- **Full Telemetry:** Syncs cycling sessions including duration, distance, calories, GPS routes, heart rate, power, cadence, and speed.
- **Privacy-First & Secure:** Processes `.fit` files entirely in-memory—no tracking, no third-party data collection, and no local disk caching of raw activity files.
- **Background Sync:** Automatically keeps your Health Connect data up-to-date in the background.
